Common Questions

Will one system cover my whole hotel?

Yes. D'Cal is fitted on your main water pipe. We size it to your rooms, guests and water hardness. So every room, the kitchen, laundry and boiler get soft water from one system.

Does it need electricity or salt?

No. D'Cal uses no electricity and needs no salt or chemicals. So there is no running cost and nothing for your staff to do daily.

How much does it cost? When do I get it back?

The price depends on your hotel size and water hardness. Most hotels get the cost back in a few months. They save on laundry, power, repairs and new parts. We give you exact numbers after a free water test.

Will fitting it stop my hotel work?

No. We plan the work around your hotel. It is done fast on the main pipe at a good time for you, with little downtime.

What is the "free hotel water test"?

Our expert comes to your hotel. He tests your water, checks your taps and boilers, and gives you a clear report. It shows what hard water costs you and which system you need. It is free, with no pressure to buy.

Do you help resorts and hotel chains?

Yes. Luxury hotels, resorts, budget hotels, lodges, service apartments and hotel chains. We size and help each place on its own.
